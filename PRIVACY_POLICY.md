# FRESCO — Privacy Policy
**Last Updated: November 2026**

Welcome to **FRESCO** ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of our users. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, process, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
We collect only the information necessary to provide you with produce freshness assessment and inventory shelf life predictions:

1. **Account Information**: When you register, we collect your username, email address, and an encrypted password hash. Guest users can explore the app without providing personal identifying information.
2. **Camera & Photos**: When you use the scanning feature, our application requests access to your device camera and/or photo library to capture images of produce. Uploaded images are processed solely to determine fruit classification, freshness score, and shelf life.
3. **Inventory & Preferences**: Items you save to your fridge or counter inventory, storage methods, custom notes, and preference settings (notification preferences, weight units).
4. **Push Notification Tokens**: If you enable notifications, we collect your Expo push device token so our background worker can send you timely alerts before produce expires.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use your information exclusively to:
- Run AI inference to predict shelf life and ethylene storage compatibility.
- Track fruit expiration dates and send notifications you requested.
- Maintain and secure your personal inventory.
- Improve AI model accuracy and system reliability.

We do **not** sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or produce photos to third parties or advertising networks.

---

### 3. Data Storage & Security
- All communications between the mobile application and backend servers use encrypted HTTPS / TLS protocols.
- Passwords are salted and hashed using industry-standard bcrypt.
- Cloud object storage for images utilizes access-controlled Amazon S3 / Cloudflare R2 buckets.

---

### 4. User Rights & Account Deletion (App Store Guideline 5.1.1)
You have full control over your personal data:
- **Account Deletion**: You can permanently delete your account and all associated inventory and notifications directly from the app by navigating to **Account & Settings > Account Actions > Delete Account**.
- **Data Export & Requests**: You may contact our privacy team at `privacy@frescoapp.com` to request a copy of your stored records or ask questions regarding our privacy practices.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy
FRESCO does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

---
